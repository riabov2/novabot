Trial & licenses

Account access, trial activation and license keys are managed through the existing Customer Hub.

Free trial

Eligible accounts can activate one 30-minute trial, create a trial game slot and connect NovaBot Agent.

Paid licenses

One license key is assigned to one game slot and one active emulator. Paid pricing is confirmed through NovaBot support before purchase.

Activate a key

Sign in to the Hub, enter the full license key and use the newly created game slot. Existing device-transfer limits continue to apply.
