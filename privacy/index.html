Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Information we handle

NovaBot may process account email addresses, password hashes, session records, license and game-slot records, device binding identifiers, Agent connection status, security logs, and the settings or game-state data needed to provide the Customer Hub.

Why we use it

We use this information to create and secure accounts, provide licensed access, connect a game slot to its authorized device, operate remote controls, prevent abuse, diagnose service faults and maintain an audit trail.

Marketing site analytics

The marketing site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate visits and page use. Google may set cookies or process technical information under its own policies. Analytics does not replace the NovaBot account system.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers used to host, protect and monitor the service. We do not describe personal data as being sold. Legal disclosure may occur when required by applicable law.

Security

NovaBot uses separate account sessions, license checks and device credentials. No online service can promise absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and report suspected access promptly.

Retention and deletion

Operational and security records are retained only as needed for the service, fraud prevention and legal obligations. License records may be retained as an audit history even after access expires or is revoked. Contact support to request an account review or deletion where applicable.

Your choices

You may sign out, change your password from the Customer Hub and contact support about your account information. Analytics choices can also be controlled through browser settings and available Google controls.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the current channels on the NovaBot contact page.
