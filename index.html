FAQ

Before you connect.

Answers about the Hub, licenses and supported devices.

Where do I control NovaBot?

All customer controls are in the NovaBot Customer Hub at app.novabotmu.com.

Does each bot need a license?

Yes. One license is assigned to one game slot and one active emulator.

Can I manage several game accounts?

Yes. Your Hub account can contain several independently controlled licensed game slots.

What device does NovaBot use?

NovaBot Agent runs on a supported rooted Android environment and connects securely to your Hub.

Can I try it first?

Eligible new accounts can create a 30 minute trial slot directly in the Customer Hub.
